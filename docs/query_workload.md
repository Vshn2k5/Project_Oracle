# Oracle Query Workload v1

These are the representative queries that Project Oracle must answer, guiding the ontology and extraction logic for the knowledge graph.

## Risk
1. Which areas are exposed to high flood risk?
2. What flood hazards affect a particular zone?
3. What infrastructure is vulnerable to flooding in Zone A?
4. What is the historical severity of urban flooding in this district?

## Infrastructure
5. Which infrastructure assets are affected by flooding?
6. What infrastructure improvements are recommended?
7. Which infrastructure projects address a specific flood risk?
8. Are there pump stations located in high-risk zones?

## Mitigation
9. What mitigation measures are recommended for a particular risk?
10. Which mitigation measures are applicable to a particular area?
11. What drainage improvements reduce flood severity?

## Budget
12. Which interventions require funding?
13. Which projects are associated with specific budget allocations?
14. What infrastructure projects should be considered under a given budget constraint?
15. Has funding been approved for the proposed canal expansion?

## Policy / Compliance
16. Which policies regulate a proposed intervention?
17. What requirements apply to a particular infrastructure project?
18. Which policies require specific mitigation measures?

## Evidence / Provenance
19. What evidence supports this recommendation?
20. Where did this information originate?
